Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: your practice answers stay in your own browser unless you choose to share them. If you sign in, we store your Google name, email and profile photo, plus anything you post to the community. We never sell your data or show ads.

Product Reps is a free interview-practice app for aspiring product managers, run by Kartik Manral (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect when you use product-reps-lhcq.vercel.app and why.

What we collect

DataWhenWhere it's kept
Your name, email address and profile photo from GoogleWhen you sign in with GoogleOur database (Supabase)
Questions, answers and comments you post, and your votesWhen you choose to post or voteOur database, visible to everyone
A daily count of your AI requestsWhen you use AI feedback or new AI questionsOur database
Reports you submit about other postsWhen you tap ReportOur database, visible only to admins
Practice drafts, scores, streaks, AI feedback results and which reading-list items you marked as readAs you practiceOnly your own browser (local storage). We never receive it.

We only request the basic Google sign-in permissions (your name, email and photo). We never access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your name and photo next to things you post.
  • To show community questions, shared answers, comments and vote counts.
  • To limit AI requests per person per day, so the free service stays available to everyone.
  • To review reported posts and remove spam or abuse.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

AI features

When you tap Get AI feedback, the question and the answer you wrote are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate feedback. When you tap New AI question, the question type, difficulty and a list of existing question texts are sent. Your name and email are not sent with these requests.

This app uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Please don't include personal or confidential information in answers you send for AI feedback. See Google's Gemini API terms for details.

Answer by voice

If you use Answer by voice, your browser's built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. In Chrome and Edge this is done by the browser maker's speech service (for Chrome, Google), under their privacy policy. Product Reps never receives, records or stores your audio; we only see the text that appears in your answer box, which stays in your browser like any typed answer. Speaking stats (pace, filler words, speaking time) are calculated in your browser and not saved.

What's public

Anything you post to the community (questions, shared answers and comments) is public, along with your Google display name and profile photo. Anyone visiting the site can see it, even without signing in. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Services we use

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Vercel: website hosting and Vercel Web Analytics. Like any web host, it processes basic request data such as IP addresses to serve the site. Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and doesn't identify you personally.
  • Google: Google sign-in and the Gemini API for AI features.

We don't use advertising trackers or tracking cookies. Sign-in uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

Your choices

  • Use it without signing in: you can practice, use the timer and browse the community without an account.
  • Delete your posts: you can delete any question, answer or comment you posted at any time from inside the app.
  • Delete your account: email us and we'll delete your account and everything linked to it within 30 days.
  • Clear local data: clearing this site's data in your browser removes your drafts, scores and streaks.
  • Revoke Google access: you can remove Product Reps at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention

We keep your account and posts until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Daily AI usage counts are only used for rate limiting.

Children

Product Reps is meant for college students and early-career professionals. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
